INTELLIGENT ARCHITECTURE

The Pulseroute Ecosystem

An end-to-end cyber-physical platform transforming static city intersections into an adaptive, self-clearing emergency transit corridor.

STEP 01 // DISPATCH

Call Center AI Integration

Intelligent triage and instant fleet activation without manual operator friction.

  • Integrated AI Telephony: Automatically transcribes and extracts caller emergency tier and incident details.
  • Instant Geolocation Fetching: Pins patient location instantly via cellular tower & GPS triangulation.
  • Optimal Fleet Dispatch: Calculates fastest arrival vector based on real-time vehicle equipment & proximity.
  • Automated Station Siren Trigger: Simultaneously activates in-station sirens and lights for immediate crew roll-out.
STEP 02 // COCKPIT

In-Vehicle HUD & Telemetry

High-refresh situational dashboard purpose-built for high-speed emergency navigation.

  • Signals Remaining Counter: Real-time countdown of upcoming intersections until scene arrival.
  • Dynamic Patient ETA: Machine-learning ETA updated every 500ms using continuous telemetry.
  • Signal Health & Phase Status: Live color, time-to-green, and controller handshake for every intersection ahead.
  • Driver Override Toggle: One-tap cockpit switch for direct manual override of any linked intersection.
STEP 03 // V2I CONTROL

V2I Preemption (700–800m)

Automated intersection control that preempts traffic before sirens are even heard.

  • Long-Range C-V2X / DSRC Handshake: Secures two-way connectivity 700 to 800 meters ahead of the intersection.
  • AI Dynamic Phase Lock: Automatically opens the transit lane green while safely switching cross-traffic to red.
  • Instant Traffic Re-Normalization: Resets signals to normal cycle the exact instant the vehicle clears the intersection.
  • 120-Second Anti-Congestion Flush: Retains subsequent signal green times for 2 minutes to eliminate accumulated gridlock.
STEP 04 // VISUAL GUIDANCE

Dynamic Intersection Guidance

Proactive visual communication projecting instructions directly to surrounding civilian drivers.

  • Digital Signal Display Panels: Ultra-bright dynamic signage integrated into traffic light gantries.
  • Proactive ETA Projection: Flashes "AMBULANCE APPROACHING: ETA 45 SECONDS" to alert waiting motorists.
  • Adaptive Lane Clear Arrows: Displays Right, Left, and revolutionary 'CLEAR MIDDLE LANE' directional vectors.
  • Driver-Controlled Path Selection: Paramedic driver selects intended lane split, instantly updating civilian displays.

CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE DEFENSE

Zero-Trust & Hardened Security

Because a traffic control preemption system is a prime target for malicious interference, Pulseroute incorporates military-grade encryption, penetration testing, and layered telemetry.

Encrypted Dual-Channel V2I & Satellite

All telemetry between vehicles and intersection nodes is encapsulated in AES-256-GCM encryption with dynamic rotating keys. High-availability failsafe incorporates redundant secure satellite links alongside terrestrial V2I mesh, rendering road cameras secondary for verification rather than single points of failure.

AES-256 + ECDSA Signatures

Continuous Penetration Testing

Rigorous adversarial vulnerability assessments, black-box penetration tests, and anti-spoofing algorithms prevent man-in-the-middle attacks, false GPS broadcasts, or unauthorized civilian signal tampering.

Zero-Day Vulnerability Auditing

Hardware Root of Trust & Anti-Tamper

Each in-vehicle cockpit unit and roadside cabinet features a dedicated Hardware Security Module (HSM). If physical tampering or firmware spoofing is detected, the unit self-isolates and alerts the central cyber operations center.

FIPS 140-3 Validated HSM
